# Silent Zero wiki

> What the sources behind the Silent Zero paper show, page by page: designs, concepts, systems and sources, each with what is proven, tested or assumed.

This wiki gathers what the sources behind the paper show. It has a page for each source, each concept, each system the paper builds on and each part of the design: 129 sources, 20 concepts, 22 systems and 8 parts of the design so far. Every page says what is proven, tested or assumed, and on which source that rests.

## How this wiki is made

A language model writes the wiki and keeps it up to date from the sources in the project's research pool, after Andrej Karpathy's idea of a wiki that a language model maintains. A daily run adds new sources and works older ones in.

- **Draft.** Written, not yet reviewed, or held back for a stated reason.
- **LLM reviewed.** A second, independent pass found every figure and every quotation in its source and read the page's claims against the sources. This is the state of most pages.
- **Human curated.** Someone on the team has also compared the page with its sources.

A page can still be wrong or misleading in detail. If you find a mistake, please [open an issue](https://github.com/entresol/silent-zero/issues). Wiki rules are noted [here](https://github.com/entresol/silent-zero/blob/main/wiki/CLAUDE.md), changes are followed [here](https://github.com/entresol/silent-zero/blob/main/wiki/log.md).


## Design

- [The kernel](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/design/kernel.html.md) · seL4 with MCS as the Silent Zero kernel: proven (C-level correctness; MCS functional correctness on RISC-V), not stated for MCS (integrity, confidentiality), tested (HACMS), assumed (boot, IOMMU); no WCET bound on any 64-bit core yet; the roadmap dates MCS on AArch64 to Q3 2027. 20 sources.
- [The static system](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/design/static-system.html.md) · one description, one image: what Microkit checks, what is not proved, and the run-time swap in LionsOS, the kernel underneath. 6 sources.
- [The broker](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/design/broker.html.md) · the only component that hands out authority: capability patterns, CaMeL and FIDES, why model-based defences fail, three agent-OS designs for the same job, CaMeL between agents; nothing proven yet; the industry's agent sandboxes grant by resource. 31 sources.
- [Real-time loops](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/design/real-time-loops.html.md) · the loops that move the motors: asynchronous chunks from slow models, policies trained for a delay range, observed timings, no proven bound yet. 16 sources.
- [The safety path](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/design/safety-path.html.md) · Simplex in Silent Zero: the agent proposes, the loop disposes; tested, not proven; the paper's 2001 date disputed by a 1994 origin; certified real-time kernels sell the same split as products. 16 sources.
- [Root of trust and firmware](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/design/root-of-trust.html.md) · the firmware below the kernel on Arm, RISC-V, PC and Raspberry Pi; OpenTitan, Caliptra, DICE; all *assumed* in the paper's terms. 13 sources.
- [The bootloader](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/design/bootloader.html.md) · where the Silent Zero loader sits on each platform, signed and measured boot, proofs about boot code so far; the loader itself unproven. 16 sources.
- [Drivers](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/design/drivers.html.md) · user-mode drivers, the first verified Ethernet driver, DMA and the IOMMU as assumed, attacked in 2019 although enabled; device models from Verilog and Windows on x86-64 as first steps; the Pancake paper adds the language and its limits. 13 sources.

- [Cohesix](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/systems/cohesix.html.md) · seL4-based control layer for edge AI fleets: a Queen decides what hosts' models may do; landing page only. 1 source.
- [KataOS](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/systems/kataos.html.md) · Google's seL4 and Rust OS for machine-learning devices, 2022; OpenTitan in its Sparrow plan. 1 source.
- [CHERIoT](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/systems/cheriot.html.md) · CHERI capabilities on small RISC-V cores with its own RTOS: memory safety in hardware, measured, not proven; CHERI for RISC-V "being standardised" in 2026. 2 sources.
- [CVA6](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/systems/cva6.html.md) · open 64-bit RISC-V core whose design lets latency bounds be proved; fence.t prototype. 7 sources.
- [SiFive and the HiFive boards](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/systems/sifive-hifive.html.md) · SiFive and its boards: the discontinued HiFive Unleashed with seL4's MCS proof, the HiFive Premier P550 one can buy. 5 sources.
- [NVIDIA Halos for Robotics](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/systems/nvidia-halos.html.md) · NVIDIA's safety stack for robots: IGX Thor with a safety island, Halos Core with Linux or Linux plus QNX; inspected for certification readiness, no robotics certificate claimed. 3 sources.
- [QNX](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/systems/qnx.html.md) · commercial microkernel RTOS, the safety side of Halos on IGX Thor; leader in an analyst's ranking for robot safety. 3 sources.
- [PikeOS](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/systems/pikeos.html.md) · commercial separation kernel and hypervisor; IEC 61508 SIL 3 and ISO 26262 ASIL D listed by the vendor; untrusted ROS 2 beside certified control. 3 sources.
- [OpenShell](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/systems/openshell.html.md) · open-source sandbox runtime for agents: Landlock, seccomp, network namespaces and a policy engine; outbound traffic denied by default; credentials as placeholders; a Developer Preview in OpenShift AI 3.5. 3 sources.
- [Microsoft Execution Containers (MXC)](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/systems/mxc.html.md) · Microsoft's policy-driven containment for agents on Windows, macOS and Linux, generally available as of 7 October 2026, with OpenShell integrated; no assurance claimed. 3 sources.

- [Simplex architecture](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/concepts/simplex.html.md) · a simple safe controller guarding an untrusted one; tested with learned controllers. 4 sources.
- [Action chunking](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/concepts/action-chunking.html.md) · slow robot models emitting action sequences for a fast loop; RTC and three 2026 policy-side answers; no bound on delay. 7 sources.
- [AI vulnerability discovery](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/concepts/ai-vulnerability-discovery.html.md) · AI models finding software flaws at scale: DARPA's challenge and Anthropic's figures, the bottleneck moving to patching. 3 sources.
- [Information-flow control](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/concepts/information-flow-control.html.md) · labels for where data came from and who may see it; FIDES and CaMeL for agents; explicit flows only; refusals as a channel; Agent libOS. 8 sources.
- [Proof automation](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/concepts/proof-automation.html.md) · machines proposing proof steps, Isabelle checking them; 77.6 % of a seL4 benchmark sample; Verus as a faster verifier. PROMISE as a second method; AutoVerus for Rust; a coding agent's compiler proofs in Lean; the OSDI version and the Selene benchmark; specification writing (OSVBench), AutoRocq, and verified against checked compiler passes. 14 sources.
- [Memory-safe code](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/concepts/memory-safe-code.html.md) · new code in Rust instead of C: Android's memory-safety flaws below 20 % in 2025; a Rust microkernel without hardware isolation as a research rival; the hardening rival to a verified base. 2 sources.
- [Mixed criticality](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/concepts/mixed-criticality.html.md) · software of different criticality on one platform; processor time as a capability in seL4 MCS; partitions under a hypervisor in commercial robot stacks. 7 sources.
- [Model Context Protocol](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/concepts/model-context-protocol.html.md) · the open protocol that connects agents to tools; three preprints measure its security, one runtime treats it as a client only. 5 sources.
- [Agent operating systems](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/concepts/agent-operating-systems.html.md) · designs that put the limit on an agent in the OS layer: agentOS, Cohesix, KataOS, AgentKernel, AgenticOS, Agent libOS, Quine, OpenShell and MXC as shipped products; IBM's Agent-OS position paper; IntentOS, a language-model planner beside the Linux kernel. 14 sources.
