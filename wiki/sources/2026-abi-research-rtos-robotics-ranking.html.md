## What it shows

- **A market category with named leaders.** The firm names QNX, Wind River, SYSGO and Green Hills Software as leaders in a ranking of commercial RTOS for robotics functional safety. The subtitle says vendors "with certified architectures, mixed-criticality support, and deep ecosystem partnerships are gaining a competitive edge".
- **Reasons given.** QNX led for its microkernel architecture, extensive safety certifications and ecosystem; Wind River had the top implementation score, with its VxWorks installed base; SYSGO's PikeOS stood out for its separation-kernel and hypervisor approach to mixed-criticality robotics ([PikeOS](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/systems/pikeos.html.md)); Green Hills was recognised for a high-assurance, security-first architecture.
- **Trends the analyst names.** Stricter functional-safety standards, AI-ready robotics platforms, demand for edge-to-cloud safety integration, wider adoption of mixed-criticality architectures with deterministic scheduling and hypervisor support, and a convergence of functional safety and cybersecurity.

## How

An analyst's judgement summarised in a press release. No method, scores or sample.

## Limits

The ranking, its criteria and scores were not read. An analyst's ranking is evidence that a category and a market exist, not of the quality of any product. The release says nothing about formal verification.
