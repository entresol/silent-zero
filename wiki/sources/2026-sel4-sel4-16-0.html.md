# seL4 Foundation: the news page, January to October 2026

> The seL4 news page for 2026: releases 15.0.0, 16.0.0 and 17.0.0 with their breaking changes, the AArch64 confidentiality item, Foundation members. report, full text.

Source · LLM reviewed · updated 2026-11-22 · https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/sources/2026-sel4-sel4-16-0.html

The seL4 Foundation's news page, read on its state of early November 2026. The pool entry is titled "seL4 16.0.0 release", but the page it points to is the whole 2026 news list: release announcements, membership news and summit items. [News](https://sel4.systems/news/). The page is the Foundation's own statement and gives no proofs, only announcements.

## What it shows

- **Three releases in 2026.** seL4 15.0.0 with Microkit 2.2.0 on 31 March, seL4 16.0.0 with Microkit 2.3.0 on 22 July, and seL4 17.0.0 with Microkit 2.4.0 on 30 October. Each announcement lists CAmkES, capDL and rust-sel4 releases next to the kernel.
- **15.0.0.** New features; code that uses the seL4 API directly needs updates. CAmkES and capDL gain domain schedules, and applications that use the domain scheduler need updates. Microkit 2.2.0 should need no change to user code compared with 2.1.0.
- **16.0.0.** Breaking changes for direct users of the seL4 API and of the Rust support. CAmkES and capDL add units for domain schedules and stay compatible. Microkit 2.3.0 brings new features and has breaking changes for x86_64 systems.
- **17.0.0.** The announcement says the release "focussed on fixing issues in unverified configurations of seL4", and on auditing code in verified configurations that the formal proofs do not cover. It carries security-relevant changes and recommends upgrading. It thanks Anthropic for reporting a number of the issues and says the audit "has strengthened the security of seL4 in all parts of the kernel that the formal proofs do not make guarantees about". 17.0.0 and the Rust support have breaking changes; Microkit 2.4.0 has security and bug fixes and breaking changes.
- **Security proofs on AArch64, 24 August.** Proofcraft has proved that seL4 enforces confidentiality on AArch64, after the proofs of functional correctness and integrity. The item calls this the completion of the proof that the implementation code on AArch64 enforces security isolation of applications, "under the assumptions listed" on a linked page.
- **Foundation news.** Riverside Research (19 January), Fraunhofer AISEC (28 January), Neutrality (13 March), Gapfruit (1 June) and the Australian National University (18 September, as Associate Member) joined. The seL4 Summit 2026 was announced for 1 to 3 September in Vancouver, with an informal social on Monday 31 August; on 16 September the Foundation published videos and slides and called the event a success. The 29 July item marks the day in 2009 on which the last "sorry" of the functional-correctness proof was removed.

## How

News items written by the Foundation. The page was read as a whole; the sponsor thank-you items and the summit programme items were read for facts and skipped for detail. The pool holds a gzip-compressed snapshot, dated 2 November 2026 in its file name.

## Limits

- It is an announcement list. A release item says what a release focused on, and gives neither the list of issues nor their severity; the notes are on the docsite.
- The AArch64 item points to a list of assumptions that this page does not hold.
- A careful reader adds: the page has one item on MCS proofs (29 June: functional correctness of MCS seL4 completed on RISC-V by Proofcraft, to be ported to 64-bit Arm under DARPA's PROVERS programme), which matches the wiki, and nothing on timing or multicore.

## Against the wiki

- [seL4](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/systems/sel4.html.md): **extends**. It gives the 2026 release dates and the content of 15.0.0 and 16.0.0, which the page lacked; the AArch64 confidentiality item is the one the page already cites through [security proofs complete on AArch64](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/sources/2026-proofcraft-sel4-security-proofs.html.md).
- [release 17.0.0](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/sources/2026-sel4-release-17.html.md): **consistent on the release**, with the AArch64 difference above. It reads the release notes and the caveats of 17.0.0; this page carries the announcement and the thanks to Anthropic. The tension the kernel page records, between the 24 August item (AArch64 confidentiality proved) and the caveats file's "in progress" for AArch64 with hypervisor extensions, is not resolved by this page; the 24 August item does not mention hypervisor extensions, so the two may concern different configurations (our reading), see [The kernel](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/design/kernel.html.md).
- [Microkit](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/systems/microkit.html.md): **extends**. Microkit 2.3.0 is announced with breaking changes for x86_64 systems; the page already covers 2.4.0.
- [AI vulnerability discovery](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/concepts/ai-vulnerability-discovery.html.md): **related**. The announcement thanks a model developer for reporting a number of the issues it fixed and audited; it does not say which belong to unverified configurations and which to unproved parts of verified ones.

## For Silent Zero

[The kernel](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/design/kernel.html.md): no change of state. Our reading: the cadence, three kernel releases in seven months with breaking changes announced for the last two, is a cost for a design that pins one release; the news items give no statement on long-term support.
