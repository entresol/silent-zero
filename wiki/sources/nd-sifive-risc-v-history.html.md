# SiFive: RISC-V History

> SiFive’s account of RISC-V and of itself. report, full text.

Source · LLM reviewed · updated 2026-10-31 · https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/sources/nd-sifive-risc-v-history.html

A page of the company SiFive, undated, read on 31 October 2026. [Page](https://www.sifive.com/about/risc-v-history).

## What it shows

- **Who SiFive is.** "Founded by the inventors of RISC-V": the page names Krste Asanović, Andrew Waterman and Yunsup Lee as SiFive's founders, who created RISC-V "working with David Patterson".
- **Timeline, as SiFive gives it.** 2010, RISC-V invented in Berkeley; 2015, SiFive founded, and RISC-V International founded; 2016, "first commercial RISC-V SoC and first RISC-V board, the HiFive1"; 2022, a valuation of $2.5 billion; 2025, more than 2 billion devices based on SiFive designs and 400 design wins; 2026, a valuation of $3.65 billion.
- **What SiFive sells.** Processor core IP, plus software and tools.
- **Profiles.** Customisation can fragment RISC-V; profiles define sets of instructions software can rely on. RVA23 targets 64-bit application processors running rich operating systems.
- **RISC-V International** has "more than 4500 members" and its headquarters in Switzerland, by SiFive's account.

## How

## Limits

Everything here is SiFive's own statement. The wiki has not checked the valuations, the device counts or the description of RISC-V as "one of the three major ISAs" against independent sources. A careful reader adds: RISC-V International dates its own founding to 2015 as the RISC-V Foundation ([RISC-V International: About](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/sources/nd-riscv-international-about.html.md)); the page's "RISC-V International founded" in 2015 refers to that foundation.
