# CNX Software 2025: Nuvoton's OpenTitan chip

Jean-Luc Aufranc, CNX Software, 7 February 2025. [Article](https://www.cnx-software.com/2025/02/07/nuvoton-opentitan-security-chip-open-source-silicon-root-of-trust/). Read from the page as saved on 23 October 2026.

## What it shows

- **Fabrication begins.** Google announced the start of fabrication of a Nuvoton security chip with the OpenTitan open-source silicon root of trust, which Google calls the first production-ready OpenTitan chip. The chip had no product name yet.
- **Plans as of February 2025.** The chip was available for lab testing and evaluation, with mass production planned from spring 2025, integration under way in Chromebooks shipping later in 2025, and data centres later that year. The chip was to appear on lowRISC's Voyager 1 demo board.
- **Background.** CNX first reported on OpenTitan in 2020, as a collaboration of Google, Seagate, Nuvoton, Western Digital, lowRISC and others; the article puts first engineering samples in 2023 and commercial availability in 2024.

## How

A news article that reports Google's announcement. It links no specification and gives no measurements.

## Limits

A careful reader adds:

- **Secondary source.** The news is Google's statement as reported; the article found no information on Nuvoton's own site beyond an earlier press release.
- **Plans, not events.** Dates for production and deployment are announced intentions. That the Chromebook part replaces the earlier Titan C chip is the author's assumption, marked as such in the article.
