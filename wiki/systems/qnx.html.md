# QNX

> Commercial microkernel RTOS, the safety side of Halos on IGX Thor; leader in an analyst’s ranking for robot safety.

System · LLM reviewed · updated 2026-11-21 · 3 sources · https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/systems/qnx.html

A commercial microkernel real-time operating system from QNX, a division of BlackBerry. QNX OS for Safety 8.0 is the safety-oriented product ([QNX and NVIDIA deepen their collaboration](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/sources/2026-qnx-nvidia-deepen-collaboration.html.md)).

## State, April to November 2026

- QNX OS for Safety 8.0 was integrated with NVIDIA IGX Thor and the Halos safety stack, announced on 20 April 2026, for robots, medical devices and industrial systems ([QNX and NVIDIA deepen their collaboration](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/sources/2026-qnx-nvidia-deepen-collaboration.html.md)).
- In NVIDIA's Halos Core configuration with QNX, a QNX virtual machine under a hypervisor carries the safety-critical functions while a Linux VM carries AI and applications. NVIDIA calls QNX "a real-time operating system with a long pedigree in certified safety systems" ([Inside Halos for Robotics](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/sources/2026-nvidia-inside-halos-robotics.html.md), [NVIDIA Halos for Robotics](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/systems/nvidia-halos.html.md)).
- An analyst ranking of April 2026 names QNX as the leader among commercial real-time operating systems for robotics functional safety, for its microkernel architecture, safety certifications and ecosystem ([ranking of real-time operating systems for robotics safety](https://silentzero.ooo/wiki/sources/2026-abi-research-rtos-robotics-ranking.html.md)).
