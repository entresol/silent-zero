Get involved

We are looking for people who know firmware, formal verification or robots, and who want to test this argument before the architecture hardens around it.

  • Discuss an idea or ask a question: Discussions.
  • See what the sources behind the paper show, page by page: the wiki.
  • Point out a flaw, a wrong claim or a missing source: Issues.
  • Propose a change to the paper or the code: a pull request; CONTRIBUTING.md says how we work.
